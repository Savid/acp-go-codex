---
title: "Observability"
description: "OpenTelemetry signals emitted by the adapter."
---

Use `WithTracerProvider`, `WithMeterProvider`, and `WithTextMapPropagator` to
connect adapter traces and metrics to the host. Prompt and tool contents are not
recorded by default. Runtime startup observations cover configuration,
process start, readiness, and session readiness without exposing native process
inventories.
